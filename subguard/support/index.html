Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a subscription?

Tap the "+" button, enter the service name, cost, billing cycle, and next billing date, then tap Save.

Will SubGuard remind me before I'm billed?

Yes. SubGuard sends a notification 3 days before each subscription renews. Make sure notifications are enabled in your device settings.

Can I track subscriptions in different currencies?

SubGuard supports multiple currencies. When adding a subscription, tap the currency symbol to choose your currency.

My total cost looks wrong

SubGuard normalizes all costs to monthly equivalents for the total. Annual subscriptions are divided by 12. Check your subscription details to verify the billing cycle is set correctly.

How do I export my subscription list?

Go to Settings → Export Data to save a CSV file of your subscriptions.

How do I manage my SubGuard Premium subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → SubGuard.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → SubGuard.
