SubGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

SubGuard is a subscription tracker app developed by the Abraham Oladotun Foundation NFP ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

SubGuard stores all your subscription data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal data on external servers.

  • Subscription names, amounts, billing dates, and notes are stored locally.
  • No account creation or login is required.
  • Your financial data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: The free version displays ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages premium subscriptions via Apple App Store or Google Play. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The free version of SubGuard shows ads via Google AdMob. Upgrade to SubGuard Premium to remove all ads. On iOS, you may be asked for App Tracking Transparency permission.

In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not handle payment information directly.

Children's Privacy

SubGuard is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can clear all subscription data from Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
