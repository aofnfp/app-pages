MealMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

MealMate is an AI-powered meal planning app developed by the Abraham Oladotun Foundation NFP ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

MealMate stores your meal plans, dietary preferences, and saved recipes locally on your device by default.

  • Dietary preferences and restrictions are stored locally.
  • Generated meal plans and saved recipes are stored on your device.
  • No account creation is required for basic functionality.

AI Meal Planning

MealMate uses AI to generate personalized meal plans. When you request a meal plan, your dietary preferences (e.g., vegetarian, nut-free) are sent to the AI service to generate suggestions. This data is used only to generate your request and is not stored by us.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: The free version displays ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages premium subscriptions. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

MealMate Premium is processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not handle payment information directly.

Children's Privacy

MealMate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can clear all meal data from Settings → Clear Data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
