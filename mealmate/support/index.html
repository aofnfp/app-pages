Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate a meal plan?

Tap "Generate Plan" on the home screen, select your dietary preferences and number of days, and tap Generate. Your personalized meal plan will appear in seconds.

Can I customize the generated meal plan?

Yes. Tap any meal in your plan to swap it for an alternative, or tap the refresh icon to regenerate that specific meal.

How do I save a recipe?

Tap any recipe to open it, then tap the bookmark icon to save it to your recipe collection.

I have dietary restrictions — will MealMate respect them?

Yes. Set your dietary preferences in Settings (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, etc.) and all generated plans will respect your restrictions.

How do I generate a shopping list?

On your meal plan screen, tap "Shopping List" to automatically compile all ingredients from your plan into a checklist you can use at the store.

How do I manage my MealMate Premium subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → MealMate.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → MealMate.
