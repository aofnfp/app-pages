AquaPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

AquaPulse is a hydration tracker app developed by the Abraham Oladotun Foundation NFP ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

AquaPulse stores all your hydration data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal data to external servers.

  • Daily water intake logs are stored locally on your device.
  • Your hydration goals, body weight (used for goal calculation), and preferences are stored on your device.
  • No account creation is required.

Health Data

AquaPulse may request permission to read/write health data (such as water intake) from Apple Health or Google Fit. This data is only used within the app and is subject to Apple's or Google's privacy policies. We do not access this data from our servers.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: The free version displays ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages premium subscriptions. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

AquaPulse Premium is processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not handle payment information directly.

Children's Privacy

AquaPulse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can clear all hydration data from Settings → Clear Data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
