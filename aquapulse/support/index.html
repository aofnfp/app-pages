Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log water intake?

Tap the "+" button or any quick-add button (e.g., 250ml, 500ml) on the home screen to log a drink. Your progress bar updates immediately.

How does AquaPulse calculate my daily goal?

AquaPulse uses your body weight (optional) and activity level to suggest a daily goal. You can override this in Settings → Daily Goal.

Can I add custom drink sizes?

Yes. Go to Settings → Custom Drinks to add your frequently used containers with their exact volumes.

AquaPulse isn't syncing with Apple Health

Go to your device Settings → Privacy → Health → AquaPulse and make sure both read and write permissions are enabled.

How do I set hydration reminders?

Go to Settings → Reminders and set the frequency and time range for your hydration nudges.

How do I manage my AquaPulse Premium subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → AquaPulse.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → AquaPulse.
