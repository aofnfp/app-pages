Privacy Policy for PageMark

Effective Date: March 29, 2026

Data Collection

PageMark stores your book library, reading progress, and settings locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal data to our servers. Your reading data never leaves your phone.

Third-Party Services

PageMark uses the following third-party services:

  • Open Library API: To search and retrieve book information. Searches are sent to openlibrary.org.
  • Google AdMob: To display advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: To manage premium subscriptions. RevenueCat processes purchase transactions through Apple/Google. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

All book data, reading progress, and preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. No account creation is required. Uninstalling the app will delete all locally stored data.

Advertising

Free users see banner and interstitial ads via Google AdMob. On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before showing personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

PageMark is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app.

Contact

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@aofnfp.org
