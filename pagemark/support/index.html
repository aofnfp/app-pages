Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a book?

Tap "+" on the home screen and search by title or author. Tap a result to add it to your library, or tap "Add Manually" to enter details yourself.

How do I update my reading progress?

Open a book in your library, tap "Update Progress", and enter your current page or percentage. PageMark will track your reading history automatically.

Can I set a reading goal?

Yes. Go to Settings → Reading Goals and set a yearly book target. Your progress is displayed on the home screen.

How do I mark a book as finished?

On the book detail screen, tap "Mark as Finished". The book moves to your "Read" shelf and your streak updates.

My book search returned no results

PageMark searches Open Library for book data. Some books may not be in the database — use "Add Manually" to add them yourself.

How do I manage my PageMark Premium subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → PageMark.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → PageMark.
