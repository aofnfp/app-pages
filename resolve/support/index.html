Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new habit?

Tap the "+" button on the main screen, enter your habit name, choose a frequency (daily, weekly, or custom), and tap Save.

How do I mark a habit as complete?

Tap the circle next to your habit name on the home screen to check it off for today.

Can I set reminders for my habits?

Yes. Open a habit, tap "Reminder", and set a time. You'll receive a daily notification at that time.

My habit streak reset unexpectedly

Streaks reset if you miss a day. Make sure you check off habits before midnight. If you believe this is an error, contact us and we'll investigate.

How do I delete a habit?

Swipe left on the habit in the main list and tap "Delete". This permanently removes the habit and its history.

How do I manage my subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Resolve.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → Resolve.
