Resolve - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 28, 2026

Overview

Resolve ("we", "our", "the app") is a habit tracking application developed by the Abraham Oladotun Foundation. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

Data Stored Locally

All habit data, progress entries, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using local storage. We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal habit data on any server.

  • Habit names and configurations
  • Daily progress and check-in data
  • App settings and preferences
  • User profile information

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • We do not sell any data to third parties

Third-Party Services

Advertising (Free Tier)
The free version of Resolve displays banner advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can review Google's privacy policy.

In-App Purchases
Premium subscriptions are processed through RevenueCat, Apple App Store, or Google Play Store. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by these platforms. We do not store payment information. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Premium Subscription

Resolve offers an optional Premium subscription that provides unlimited habit tracking, advanced statistics, custom themes, and an ad-free experience. Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. You can cancel at any time through your device's subscription settings.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all your data at any time through the app's Settings screen ("Delete All Data"). Uninstalling the app will also remove all stored data.

Children's Privacy

Resolve does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request deletion of your data, and opt out of personalized advertising (via device settings).
