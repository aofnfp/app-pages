Frequently Asked Questions

Does the timer keep running when my phone is locked?

Yes. FocusFlow uses background audio to keep the timer running even when your phone is locked or you switch to another app.

How do I cancel a session?

Tap the reset button below the timer to cancel the current session and return to the idle state.

What's included in FocusFlow Premium?

Premium unlocks all ambient sounds, removes ads, enables premium themes, and provides detailed session analytics.

How do I manage my subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → FocusFlow.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → FocusFlow.

My session history is empty

Session history is stored locally on your device. If you reinstalled the app, your history will have reset. FocusFlow does not use cloud sync.
