Frequently Asked Questions

Do sounds keep playing when my screen locks?

Yes. DreamTone uses background audio so your sleep sounds continue playing when the screen locks or you switch apps. Make sure your device volume is not muted.

How do I set a sleep timer?

Tap the timer icon on the main screen and choose a duration. Sounds will automatically fade out and stop when the timer ends.

Can I mix multiple sounds together?

Yes — with DreamTone Premium you can layer up to 3 sounds simultaneously and adjust the volume of each independently.

What's included in DreamTone Premium?

Premium removes ads, unlocks all sounds, enables sound mixing, and provides a distraction-free listening experience.

How do I manage my subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → DreamTone.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → DreamTone.

A sound I purchased is now locked

Tap "Restore Purchases" in the app settings. Your purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored at any time.
