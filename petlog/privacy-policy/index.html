PetLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

PetLog is a pet care tracking app developed by the Abraham Oladotun Foundation NFP ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

PetLog stores all your pet care data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your personal data to external servers.

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed, birthday) are stored locally.
  • Feeding logs, vet records, weight history, and medication schedules are stored on your device.
  • No account creation is required.
  • No data is synced to the cloud by default.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: The free version displays ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages premium subscriptions. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

PetLog Premium is processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not handle payment information directly.

Children's Privacy

PetLog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete a pet profile and all its data from the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
