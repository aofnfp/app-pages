Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new pet?

Tap "Add Pet" on the home screen, fill in your pet's name, species, breed, and birthday, then tap Save.

How do I log a feeding?

Select your pet, tap "Feeding Log", then tap "+" to add a new feeding entry with the time, food type, and amount.

Can I set reminders for vet appointments?

Yes. In a pet's profile, tap "Vet Records" → "Add Appointment" and enable the reminder toggle to receive a notification.

How do I track my pet's weight over time?

In your pet's profile, tap "Weight" and add entries. PetLog will display a weight chart to track trends over time.

How do I set up medication reminders?

In a pet's profile, tap "Medications" → "Add Medication", enter the name, dosage, and frequency, and enable reminders.

How do I manage my PetLog Premium subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → PetLog.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → PetLog.
