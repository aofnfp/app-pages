Bloomly Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Bloomly is a plant care companion app developed by the Abraham Oladotun Foundation NFP ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

Bloomly stores all your plant care data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal data to external servers.

  • Plant profiles (name, species, location, notes) are stored locally.
  • Watering logs, fertilization schedules, and care history are stored on your device.
  • No account creation is required.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: The free version displays ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages premium subscriptions. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Bloomly Premium is processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not handle payment information directly.

Children's Privacy

Bloomly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete a plant profile from the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
