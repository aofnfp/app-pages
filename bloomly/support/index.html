Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a plant?

Tap "+" on the home screen, enter your plant's name and species, choose its location, and tap Save. You can also take a photo of your plant.

How do I log a watering?

Select your plant, tap "Log Watering", and confirm. Bloomly updates your plant's care history and schedules the next watering reminder.

How does Bloomly know when to water my plant?

Bloomly uses species-specific watering schedules as defaults. You can customize the watering frequency for each plant in its settings.

My plant reminder isn't showing up

Make sure notifications are enabled for Bloomly in your device settings (Settings → Notifications → Bloomly).

How do I track fertilizing?

Open a plant profile and tap "Log Fertilizer". Choose the fertilizer type and amount. Bloomly tracks fertilization history separately from watering.

How do I manage my Bloomly Premium subscription?

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Bloomly.
On Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → Bloomly.
